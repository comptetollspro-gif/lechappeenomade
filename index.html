Born in the south, built for breakdowns

Crossing the Moroccan Sahara requires more than just high clearance; it demands constant mechanical awareness. From scorching heat putting engines to the test, to fine sand penetrating filtration systems, unexpected breakdowns can happen hundreds of kilometers away from any workshop.

Based out of Marrakech with deep roots across the southern pistes, we provide reassurance and technical resilience. Whether you are driving a Land Cruiser, Defender, Patrol, or an overland truck, we keep your wheels turning.
